# Code of conduct

Rivo is a small project with one maintainer. The rules are short.

- Discuss the code, not the person. Criticism of a decision is welcome; contempt for the person
  who made it is not.
- No harassment, no slurs, no sexual attention, no doxxing, no sustained disruption. This
  applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and any space where you are representing the
  project.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they prove otherwise, and say what you
  mean plainly.
- Requests to add DRM, age-gate, paywall or geo-restriction bypasses are closed without debate.
  That is the project's scope, not a personal rejection.

The maintainer may edit, lock or close anything that breaks these rules, and may block an
account that keeps breaking them. If something happens that you would rather not report in
public, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

This is not the Contributor Covenant; it is what this project actually enforces.
